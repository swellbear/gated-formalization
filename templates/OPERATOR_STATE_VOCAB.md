Fill or point. Full text: [`docs/operator_ops/STATE_VOCAB.md`](../docs/operator_ops/STATE_VOCAB.md). Habit §E: [`docs/OPERATOR_SOFTEN_FOLD_HABIT.md`](../docs/OPERATOR_SOFTEN_FOLD_HABIT.md).

| Stamp | Use when | Softened? |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| **ADMITTED** | Operator Soften / Harden / Kill / dated-record admit is on the record | Yes (kind named) |
| **REJECTED** | Founder (or Operator under idle) rejected the board | **No** |
| **PARKED** | Board parked (often with REJECTED) | **No** |
| **HOLD Soften** | Founder GO vs reject is still **open** | Not yet |

**Hard rules**

- HOLD Soften **only** while the Founder call is open.
- After Founder reject → stamp **REJECTED/PARKED**. Never HOLD Soften pending GO.
- Lab waiting Soften on **REJECTED** = Operator **hard-stop**.
