### Soft-modal fork (when claim uses potential / could / may / should / etc.)

**In plain language:** If words like “likely,” “could,” or “should” carry the claim’s strength, say how strong you mean *before* you look for evidence. Choosing that height is not the same as meeting it.

If soft modals carry claim strength, flag early (do not wait for endpoint):
