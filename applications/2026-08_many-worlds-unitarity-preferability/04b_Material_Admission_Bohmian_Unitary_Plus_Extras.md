## Candidate Material Summary

**Source(s):**  
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Bohmian Mechanics” (Dürr / Goldstein / Zanghì tradition summary)  
- Dürr, Goldstein, Zanghì, “Bohmian Mechanics and the Meaning of the Wave Function” (quant-ph/9512031)  
- Corroboration: Brown & Wallace-type comparative points that Bohmian formalism = Schrödinger evolution **plus** corpuscle configuration / guiding equation (excess structure relative to pure-wave Everett)

**Key content / finding (concise):**  
Bohmian mechanics specifies a state \((Q,\psi)\): the wavefunction evolves by the **Schrödinger equation** (unitary for the closed system’s \(\psi\)), while particle configuration \(Q\) evolves by a **guiding equation**. The theory is not “Schrödinger equation alone”; it adds beables and guidance dynamics (and typically a quantum-equilibrium / typicality story for recovering Born statistics). Everettian / MWI programs claim to use the wavefunction (and its unitary evolution) without that added configuration+guidance package.
