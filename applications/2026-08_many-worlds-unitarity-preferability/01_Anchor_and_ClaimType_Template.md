## L₀ — Objective Anchors

List only anchors that are hard to dispute (empirical results, logical constraints, or strong intersubjective facts). Number them.

1. **Shared formal core:** Non-relativistic quantum theory includes unitary Schrödinger evolution of the state vector (or equivalent unitary dynamics on density operators for closed systems) as a central dynamical law of the standard formalism.
2. **Measurement problem (intersubjective):** There is a recognized tension between (i) universal unitary evolution and (ii) the appearance of definite measurement outcomes; this is the standard “measurement problem” that interpretations address differently — not a settled empirical anomaly with one agreed fix.
3. **Collapse theories add non-unitary resources:** Explicit collapse / spontaneous-localization theories (and textbook projection postulates used as dynamical updates) introduce a non-unitary update or stochastic modification beyond pure Schrödinger evolution for the closed system.
4. **Hidden-variable theories typically add ontology/dynamics:** Pilot-wave / Bohmian mechanics retains a wavefunction with unitary Schrödinger evolution but adds extra structure (e.g. particle positions / beables and a guidance equation). That added structure is not identical to “the Schrödinger equation alone.”
5. **Everett / MWI program claim (theory-content, not truth):** Everettian / Many-Worlds interpretations propose to retain universal unitary evolution and to recover an appearance of definite outcomes via branching / decoherence / relative-state structure, without a fundamental collapse postulate.
6. **“Only” and “preferable” are criterion-relative (logical):** Uniqueness (“the only interpretation that…”) and theoretical preferability (“therefore preferable on consistency and simplicity”) are not entailed by unitary formalism alone; they depend on (a) the comparison class of interpretations, (b) what counts as an “extra postulate,” and (c) which virtues (consistency, simplicity, empirical adequacy, etc.) are weighted how.
7. **Empirical underdetermination (intersubjective among foundations workers):** Empirically adequate collapse, hidden-variable, and Everettian frameworks are widely treated as not uniquely settled by existing ordinary laboratory quantum predictions alone (setting aside proposed speculative tests of collapse models, etc.).

## Candidate Claim or Layer Element

**Full statement of the claim / layer being evaluated:**

> The Many-Worlds Interpretation is the only interpretation of quantum mechanics that preserves full unitarity and introduces no extra dynamical or epistemic postulates beyond the Schrödinger equation; therefore it is theoretically preferable to collapse theories and hidden-variable theories on grounds of consistency and simplicity.

**Sub-parts:**
- **(U)** MWI preserves full unitarity.
- **(N)** MWI introduces no extra dynamical or epistemic postulates beyond the Schrödinger equation.
- **(O)** MWI is the **only** interpretation with (U)+(N).
- **(P)** Therefore MWI is theoretically preferable to collapse and hidden-variable theories on consistency and simplicity grounds.
