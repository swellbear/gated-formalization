- Corroborating survey points in Wallace-style decoherence-Everett expositions (decoherence as approximate, level-dependent world individuation)

**Key content / finding (concise):**  
How one designates “worlds” / preferred branches is **not** fixed by the bare mathematical formalism of pure unitary wave mechanics alone. The dominant modern Everettian strategy uses **decoherence** to characterize approximate emergent worlds; decoherence is approximate, description-level-dependent, and does not by itself supply a unique, exact world-count or exact preferred-basis rule. Philosophers of physics remain divided on whether this packaging is a mere consequence of unitary dynamics or an additional interpretive layer.
