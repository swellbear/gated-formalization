## 1. Plain-language summary

This run asked whether the Many-Worlds Interpretation is the **only** interpretation of quantum mechanics that preserves full unitarity and adds **no** extra dynamical or epistemic postulates beyond the Schrödinger equation — and whether that would make Many-Worlds **theoretically preferable** to collapse theories and hidden-variable theories on consistency and simplicity grounds.

What held up is census and scoping work, not the uniqueness slogan. A minimal comparison class (Everett / collapse / Bohmian) can be locked for evaluation. Bohmian packages add structure beyond unitary Schrödinger dynamics. Everett can be characterized, as a **program**, as adding no new dynamical law of the collapse or guidance type — that is **not** a proof of “no extra postulates whatsoever.” Domain was locked to non-relativistic quantum measurement-problem framing. Uniqueness, if scored at all here, is **C₀-relative evaluation**, not a global “only interpretation” admission.

What did **not** hold up: strong **no-extras** uniqueness, and therefore the preferability leap. Preferred-basis / decoherence packaging and Born-rule / epistemic recovery remain open forks. Phase 2 marked both as **currently intractable to close** for strong no-extras purposes. Two Experimental Generation batches (five candidates total) all **REJECT** — Amb unchanged.

**Bottom line:** Original uniqueness + preferability claim is **not** well-constrained. Application stopped **Provisional** at Amb ≈ 5.5. Amb drop from scope locks is **not** clearance of uniqueness or preferability.

> The Many-Worlds Interpretation is the only interpretation of quantum mechanics that preserves full unitarity and introduces no extra dynamical or epistemic postulates beyond the Schrödinger equation; therefore it is theoretically preferable to collapse theories and hidden-variable theories on grounds of consistency and simplicity.

**Sub-parts on record:**

- **(U)** MWI preserves full unitarity.  
- **(N)** MWI introduces no extra dynamical or epistemic postulates beyond the Schrödinger equation.

**Source / domain context:** Foundations of quantum mechanics — interpretational comparison (Many-Worlds / Everett vs collapse vs hidden-variable); theoretical virtues (unitarity, postulate-minimality, simplicity, consistency). Empirical underdetermination among empirically adequate packages is an intersubjective starting point.

**Claim type:** Mixed  
- Descriptive/structural: what packages add relative to unitary dynamics; whether MWI “preserves full unitarity” in the usual Everettian sense.  
- Classificatory / high-Amb: what counts as an “extra” postulate **(N)**; uniqueness **(O)**.  
- Normative/methodological: preferability **(P)** on consistency and simplicity.
