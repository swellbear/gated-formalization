## Candidate E1 (targets A1 — preferred-basis / decoherence packaging)

**Statement:**  
If the universal state evolves solely by the unitary Schrödinger equation on a tensor-product Hilbert space \(\mathcal{H}_S \otimes \mathcal{H}_E\), and the system–environment interaction Hamiltonian is such that only a particular “pointer” observable on \(S\) has eigenstates that remain stable under monitoring by \(E\) (einselection), then the preferred basis for branching is uniquely fixed by that dynamics alone. Therefore decoherence/einselection packaging is **not** an extra postulate beyond the Schrödinger equation; A1 closes on the “not an extra” horn.

## Candidate E2 (targets A2 — Born-rule / epistemic extras)

**Statement:**  
In a branching Everettian multiverse generated by unitary evolution, any agent whose preferences over branch-local bets satisfy a short list of decision-theoretic rationality axioms (ordering, diachronic consistency, branching-indifference / equivalent-game invariance) must act as if branch weights equal squared amplitudes. Those axioms are requirements of rationality, not physical postulates. Therefore the Born rule is recovered without extra **physical** epistemic postulates beyond the Schrödinger equation; A2 closes on the “no epistemic extras” horn (Deutsch–Wallace-style sketch).
