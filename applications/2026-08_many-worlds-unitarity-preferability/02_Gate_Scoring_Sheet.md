## 1. Cons (Consistency)

**Score:** Moderate  

**Notes / contradictions found:**

- Soft pass for a weak reading of **(U)** + contrast with collapse theories (anchors 3, 5): MWI-as-program aims at universal unitarity; collapse theories add non-unitary resources.
- Tension on **(N):** Everettian packages commonly rely on decoherence, branching ontology, and a probability/measure story (Born rule recovery). Whether those are “extra dynamical or epistemic postulates beyond the Schrödinger equation” is contested; denying they are “extra” while counting Bohmian guidance as “extra” risks inconsistent postulate-counting (anchor 6).
- Tension on **(O):** Other no-collapse approaches (e.g. some relational / relative-state variants; “relative facts” programs; certain decoherence-only instrumental stances) may compete depending on the comparison class. “Only” is fragile.
- Tension on **(P):** Even if MWI uniquely minimized dynamical postulates, preferability does not follow without adopting simplicity/consistency as decisive virtues and defining them — and without weighing costs (probability problem, preferred basis, ontology of branches) that critics treat as consistency/simplicity penalties.

## 2. Agree (Agreement / Stability)

**Score:** Low  

**Notes on stability of reading across careful passes or reviewers:**

- Foundations community is deeply split on MWI preferability; uniqueness claims are especially unstable.
- Even a single careful operator oscillates on whether Born-rule arguments, decoherence, and branch metrics are “just theorems from Schrödinger dynamics” or additional interpretive postulates.
- Agreement is higher on the narrow contrast “collapse adds non-unitarity; Bohmian adds beables+guidance” than on **(O)** or **(P)**.

## 3. Prod (Productivity)

**Number of new, non-trivial, checkable consequences:** 2  

**List them:**
1. **Postulate census discipline:** If the claim is to be constrained, one can build an explicit inventory of dynamical vs epistemic posits for MWI, GRW-type collapse, Bohmian mechanics, and at least one epistemic interpretation — then re-score **(N)/(O)** against that census (checkable as a comparative table, not as physics experiment).
2. **Virtue-criteria operationalization:** Preferability **(P)** becomes checkable only after naming simplicity/consistency metrics (e.g. postulate count; ontological commitment count; avoidance of non-unitary dynamics; treatment of probability). Different named metrics should be allowed to disagree — a productive, falsifiable structure for the meta-claim.
3. *(Borderline / not counted)* “MWI alone preserves unitarity” — too close to a definitional restatement of the Everett program unless compared against a fixed interpretation list.

**Scoring guide reminder:**  
- 0 → fails Prod  
- 1 → minimum pass  
- 2+ → strong Prod
