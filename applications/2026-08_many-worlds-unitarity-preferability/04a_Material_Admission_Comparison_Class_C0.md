## Candidate Material Summary

**Source(s):**  
- PhilPapers topic summary, “Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics” (editorial characterization of main competing responses to the measurement problem)  
- Allori et al. / collapse-models review framing (arXiv:2310.14969) contrasting Everettian, Bohmian, and dynamical-collapse approaches as the primary realist response-types  
- IEP “Quantum Mechanics, Interpretations of” (many-worlds / Bohm / GRW as distinct ontological packages)

**Key content / finding (concise):**  
Standard foundations discourse treats the main *realist* competitors on the measurement problem as (at least) three incompatible packages: **(i)** Everett / many-worlds (unitary wavefunction, no fundamental collapse, recover outcomes perspectivally / via branching), **(ii)** Bohmian / pilot-wave (unitary wavefunction **plus** additional beables + guidance dynamics), **(iii)** objective/dynamical collapse (modified non-unitary dynamics). PhilPapers explicitly notes that Bohmian and collapse approaches are often better described as **different theories** with added dynamical structure, while Everett attempts recovery without that added structure. The candidate claim’s own wording already names collapse theories and hidden-variable theories as the contrast class.
