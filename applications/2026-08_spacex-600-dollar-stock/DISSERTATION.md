## 1. Plain-language summary

This application tested the slogan: “SpaceX has potential to become a $600 stock.”

As bare text, that slogan is under-specified — “potential,” share count, and time horizon are not fixed. The operator locked a concrete reading (**Rank 1**): a **non-negligible** (live, not vanishing) path for SPCX to trade at **$600 per share by about 2036** on an **IPO-scale** share base (about **$7.9 trillion** equity value), **not** via reverse-split-only engineering.

Under that lock, the question became well-posed. After Phase 1 constraints and two Phase 2 passes (scenario partition, then deep evidence, accuracy-first), the locked **non-negligible** bar was **not established**. Independent fair-value marks sit roughly an order of magnitude below the ~$7.9T target; AI/compute lease stories look fragile; Starship marketed economics are unverified; Starlink is already in the mix as the strongest demonstrated leg and still does not close the multi-trillion gap alone. A thin bull corridor remains on paper as a conjunctive stack of optionality — that is not the same as clearing a real-shot bar.

**Bottom line:** Well-posed under Rank 1; **M2 (P-NonNegligible) not established**. Hard stop as **Stable Provisional**. This is not a proof that $600 is impossible, and not investment advice.
