## 2. Accuracy checks against ~$7.9T / M2

### Check 1 — Independent weighted value << target
Morningstar’s probability-weighted whole-firm FV (~$780B) is ~**10× below** ~$7.9T. Even treating AI as a call option, their framework does **not** center anywhere near Rank 1’s equity target.

### Check 2 — Even the named “Moonshot” ops sketch undershoots unless multiples go extreme
Optimistic independent sketch: ~**$225B** orbital-AI revenue by ~2035.  
To carry **~$7.9T** on that stream alone ⇒ sales multiple ≈ **35×** on that segment, with other segments extra — or you still need the conflicted bank-scale group revenue paths.  
Morningstar assigns that moonshot class only **~7%** weight and still lands FV at ~$780B.

### Check 3 — Near-term AI “run-rate” is not a durable $8T foundation
Headline Anthropic+Google rent ≈ **$2B+/month** if fully on. Fine print: mutual **90-day** outs (and public short-base lease framing). That supports IPO narrative optionality; it does **not** securely underwrite decade compounding to ~$8T.

### Check 4 — Starship enabler still unverified at marketed economics
Bull orbital-compute stories need cheap, high-cadence Starship. Marketed $/kg remains **aspirational**; independent work treats higher $/kg as nearer-term probable; S-1-era reporting does not show verified commercial $/kg. Enabler risk stays first-class in the conjunction.

### Check 5 — Conflicted bull curves remain the main paper bridge to ~$8T
The cleanest *arithmetic* bridges to ~$7.9T still lean on bookrunner-scale 2030–2040 revenue fantasies + rich multiples (Attempt 1 S-Bull). Accuracy pass does **not** upgrade those into non-conflicted M2 support.
