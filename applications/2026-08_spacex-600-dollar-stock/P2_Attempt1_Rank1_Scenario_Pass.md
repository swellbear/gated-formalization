## 2. Scenario set (structured)

### S-Bear — Starship / AI miss

**Story:** Starship cadence/economics slip; orbital compute delayed; xAI stays capital-intensive with weak monetization; multiple compresses toward infrastructure/telecom comps.  

**2036 equity value (order):** ~**$0.5T–$1.5T** (near or below independent FV band; MS-style bear print ~$75/share ≈ ~$1T order on IPO-scale shares).  

**Hits $600 / ~$7.9T?** **No.**

---

### S-Base — Starlink compounds; partial optionality

**Story:** Starlink remains the cash engine; some Starship success; AI grows but far below bank 2030 AI figures; market pays a large premium to trailing results but not mega-TAM perfection.  

**2036 equity value (order):** ~**$2T–$4T** (roughly 1–2× IPO mega-cap neighborhood, not 4×+).  

**Illustrative:** $150–250B 2036 revenue × 12–20× sales ≈ $1.8–$5T — can touch mid band, **strains** to clear $7.9T without richer multiple *and* higher revenue.  

**Hits $600 / ~$7.9T?** **No** (central). Edge of band only with stacked optimism.

---

### S-Bull — Bank ultra-long AI / Starship corridor

**Needed joint successes (optionality stack):**  
1. Starship reaches cheap, high-cadence reuse (enabler).  
2. Terrestrial then **orbital** AI/compute scales into hundreds of $B–$T revenue.  
3. xAI/frontier model + cloud economics sustain premium multiples.  
4. Market continues to capitalize long-dated TAM (governance/float/lockup risks contained).

**Bridge math (illustrative, not a forecast):**  
- If group revenue were ~**$400–500B** by ~2030 (GS-like) and continued compounding to ~**$800B–$1.2T** by 2036, then at **~8–12×** sales the equity value lands ~**$6.4T–$14T** — i.e. **$600 becomes reachable in the bull corridor**.  
- MS’s reported **$3.4T revenue by 2040** curve, if even partly on track by 2036 with a mid-teens sales multiple, can also intersect ~$8T.  
- MS initiation **$600 bull** shows the **price level** appears in at least one major-bank scenario fan — but that print does **not** disclose probability weight or prove a 10y ~$8T path.

**Hits $600 / ~$7.9T?** **Yes, on paper** — only if the stacked optionality mostly works **and** multiples stay rich.
