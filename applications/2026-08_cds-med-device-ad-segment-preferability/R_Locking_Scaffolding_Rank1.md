## 2. Options per decision point

### Point H — High-priority bar

| Option ID | What it means in ordinary terms | Provenance |
|-----------|---------------------------------|------------|
| **H1** | **Threshold (2-of-3):** Counts as high-priority if ≥2 of: (a) **Audience fit** — platform specialties overlap procedures/decisions where device choice matters; (b) **Buyer signal** — device manufacturers already buy HCP point-of-care / CDS-adjacent media *or* this platform has recent device-seller pipeline conversations; (c) **Ops path** — a compliance/review path for device promo on the product is identified (even if not yet used). | Standard commercial prioritization (fit × demand × feasibility) |
| **H2** | **Top-3 slate:** High-priority iff med device is in the internal top-3 expansion segments by expected year-1 pilot revenue (or expected contribution margin). | Portfolio ranking |
| **H3** | **Nomination:** High-priority if sales/GM names it as a priority for the next pilot cycle (no numeric bar). | Soft / gameable |

### Point S — Med-device scope

| Option ID | What it means in ordinary terms | Provenance |
|-----------|---------------------------------|------------|
| **S1** | **Procedure-adjacent devices:** Implantables, surgical/interventional tools, and procedure capital equipment where CDS moments (indication, technique, device selection) are plausible. Excludes pure consumer wearables. | Best narrative fit to CDS |
| **S2** | **Broad devices:** S1 + disposables, monitoring/diagnostics hardware, hospital capital not tied to a single procedure moment. | Wider TAM; weaker CDS story |
| **S3** | **Connected / digital-leaning devices only:** Software-heavy / connected devices and digital therapeutics with device components. | Narrow; different buyer motion |

### Point P — Pilot definition

| Option ID | What it means in ordinary terms | Provenance |
|-----------|---------------------------------|------------|
| **P1** | **90-day paid pilot:** 1–3 device advertisers; limited inventory; success = ≥1 paid campaign live (or signed IO that starts in-window) **and** no blocking compliance incident. | Lean commercial pilot |
| **P2** | **6-month revenue pilot:** Broader seller set; success = hit a stated pilot revenue floor (operator sets $ later as OR-slot). | Heavier bar |
| **P3** | **Discovery only:** Timeboxed outreach (e.g. ≥5 qualified device-seller meetings in 60 days); **no** inventory commitment required for “pilot success.” | Pre-pilot; weak test of Rank 1 |
