## 1. Plain-language summary

A CDS business asked whether **med device is the best next ad segment** after pharma. That “best” claim **does not clear** — it needs a named race and scoring rules. Under an operator-authorized **stipulated proxy** (multi-specialty procedural audience, category device media buying, draftable compliance path), that weaker claim **holds**. It does **not** prove the same about the live product until real data replaces the proxy, and it does **not** prove med device is best.
