## L₀ — Objective Anchors

1. A public arXiv HTML for 2603.24244 was retrievable on 2026-08-13 and can be quoted.  
2. Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSP2-4.5 vs SSP5-8.5) are **scenario labels** in CMIP6, not a completed real-world mitigation policy.  
3. A model that trains on **national** annual heat-death counts and then forces **city/state temperatures** is not the same as observed city-level deaths.  
4. Print of the authors’ own figures is not an independent forecast verification.  
5. “Demonstrate that mitigation can reduce X” is stronger than “two scenario runs diverged in this setup.”
