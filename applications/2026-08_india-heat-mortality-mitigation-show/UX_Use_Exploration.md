## 0. Plain-language framing (required)

**What the method established:**  
The 2026-08-13 HTML of [arXiv:2603.24244](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24244) **reports** a national 1970–2023 heat-death compilation, an ARIMAX model forced by summer temperatures, six CMIP6 models, 67 ~1° sites, and a steeper, more spread-out high-emission path **in that setup**. The authors flag absolute counts as uncertain and list missing humidity, coarse grids, and no city-level deaths. Their conclusion uses **could**.

**What the method blocked / did not establish:**  
That those findings **demonstrate** climate mitigation substantially reducing both the **size** and the **unevenness** of future **urban** heat-health burdens. City death maps. A real mitigation-law evaluation. An independent rerun. Heat is harmless (not claimed; not a refute).

**What this step is:** Exploring possible *uses* of that split, guided by what the abstract was trying to do (treat the model contrast as shown mitigation benefit for cities).

**What this step is not:** Forecasts, advice, “should emit / should not emit,” or method-established truth. **Core verdict unchanged** (Stable Provisional split — hard stop). Using the findings does **not** clear the demonstrate-mitigation bar.
