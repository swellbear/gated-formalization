## Identified Gaps

### Gap 1 — Debt-limit vs spending authority
**Description:** Whether pairing debt-limit action with spending cuts is required because the limit vote *is* a spending decision, or is a separate political/procedural linkage.  

**Claim-freeze:** Whether debt-limit increases/suspensions are correctly treated as the fiscal act that must be “balanced,” given that the limit primarily authorizes financing of already-enacted obligations.

**Impact:** 2 | **Anchor:** 2 | **Measurability:** 2 | **Sum:** 6
