**Claim-freeze quoted:** Whether debt-limit increases/suspensions are correctly treated as the fiscal act that must be “balanced,” given financing-of-obligations mechanics.

---

## Candidate Material Summary

**Source(s):** L₀.1–L₀.2 (standard institutional description).  
**Key content:** A debt-limit increase or suspension authorizes additional *borrowing to pay obligations already incurred* under prior law; it is not itself an appropriation of new program spending. Pairing with spending cuts is a **political/procedural linkage**, not a logical identity between “raising the limit” and “increasing spending.”
