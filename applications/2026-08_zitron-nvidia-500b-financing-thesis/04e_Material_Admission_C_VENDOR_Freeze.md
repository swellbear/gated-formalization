For scoring **C-VENDOR** in this app:

> **VF-BAR:** A financing structure counts as “vendor financing / circular in substance” if (i) capital is raised primarily to fund purchase or deployment of the **supplier’s** product (here Nvidia compute), **and** (ii) the supplier provides **material** credit, residual-value, demand, or similar support that is economically material to underwriting — as opposed to purely independent third-party underwriting with no material supplier residual/credit support.  
> **“Just”** is treated as a soft evaluative (near-total / dominant characterization), not a bare logical possibility.

Nvidia’s public denial of “circular financing” and Zitron’s affirmation are **competing classifications under VF-BAR**, not yet resolved by admitted deal-level evidence.
