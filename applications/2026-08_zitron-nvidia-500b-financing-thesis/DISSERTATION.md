## 1. Plain-language summary

Ed Zitron’s Aug 11–12 writing says Nvidia’s ~$500B Wall Street financing push is effectively **vendor/circular financing**, still largely **theoretical (MoUs)**, and is being used to support extraordinary GPU-sale expectations while buyers take on huge debt. He also argues OpenAI and Anthropic dominate hyperscaler **AI** revenue (~70%+) and that scaled demand is not sustainable without those two labs’ fundraising.

**What held up:** Nvidia **did announce** MoUs with six firms aiming to mobilize over $500 billion. **Right now** that figure is MoU/aim language — not closed funded capital or booked Nvidia revenue. We froze what “vendor/circular,” “70% concentration,” and “unsustainable demand” **mean**. We locked Zitron’s Nvidia FY/~$1.6T table and his 70% **citations** as *what he claims*.

**What did not hold up as proven:** That the package **is** vendor financing; that the FY/~$1.6T path is independent consensus fact; that OpenAI+Anthropic are ≥70% of Microsoft/Google/Amazon **AI** revenue; that demand is a “bubble” under the sustainability bar.

**Bottom line:** The announcement and present-tense MoU status are solid. The economic indictment is well-posed and **not cleared**. This is not investment advice. CNBC Squawk was **kept out** of the claim package.
