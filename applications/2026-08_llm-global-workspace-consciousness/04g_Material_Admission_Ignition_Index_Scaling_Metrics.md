## Candidate Material Summary

**Source(s):** Rahbar, “The Ignition Index: Measuring Global Workspace Dynamics in Language Models” (arXiv:2608.05160, 2026).  

**Key content / finding (concise):**  
Defines Ignition Index \(\hat{\beta}\) — sigmoid steepness of layer-wise probe accuracy vs input signal strength — as a quantitative operationalization of GWT-like all-or-none ignition dynamics in LMs. Validated with shuffled-label controls. Findings relevant here:
- Attention-based transformers show higher ignition than state-space models (architecture discriminability).
- Recurrent-depth models show axis-dependent ignition.
- Training-time phase transition in ignition observed (Pythia-410M).
- **Hypotheses linking ignition strength to model scale were not confirmed** — GWT-aligned ignition dynamics do **not** monotonically track scale; architectures may saturate available ignition mechanisms.
- Explicitly: high \(\hat{\beta}\) is a candidate indicator of global information availability, **not** a measure of consciousness / phenomenal status.
