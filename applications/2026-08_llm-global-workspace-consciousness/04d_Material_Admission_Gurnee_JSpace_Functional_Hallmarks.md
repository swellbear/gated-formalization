## Candidate Material Summary

**Source(s):** Gurnee, Lindsey, et al., “Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models” (Anthropic / Transformer Circuits, 2026); Anthropic summary “A global workspace in language models.”  

**Key content / finding (concise):**  
Using Jacobian-lens methods, the authors identify a privileged set of intermediate-layer representations (“J-space”) argued to show **functional hallmarks** associated with a global workspace / conscious access: reportability, capacity limits (order of tens of concepts), use in flexible/silent reasoning, and wider broadcast than other representations. **Authors explicitly do not claim** reproduction of full GWT brain architecture: no cleanly separable encapsulated input processors; broadcast within a **single feedforward pass** rather than recurrent loops; competition/ignition analogy unclear relative to sharp neural ignition.
