## L₀ — Objective Anchors

List only anchors that are hard to dispute (empirical results, logical constraints, or strong intersubjective facts). Number them.

1. **GWT core structure (theory content):** Global Workspace Theory (Baars; Dehaene-style neural GWT variants) posits a limited-capacity global workspace / cognitive bottleneck: selected information is broadcast for widespread access by otherwise specialized processors. This is what the theory structurally claims, independent of whether any particular AI implements it.
2. **Access vs phenomenal distinction:** In philosophy of mind it is standard to distinguish **access consciousness** (information globally available for report, reasoning, and control) from **phenomenal consciousness** (subjective experience / “what it is like”). Theories can claim one without settling the other.
3. **GWT’s primary evidential target:** Neural GWT programs primarily aim to explain access / reportability and associated signatures of ignition and widespread cortical availability in brains; whether GWT also *entails* phenomenal consciousness remains contested among theorists and is not a settled empirical fact.
4. **Current LLM architectural facts:** Dominant large language models are transformer-based systems trained largely for next-token prediction (and post-training), using multi-head attention over a finite context window, feed-forward sublayers, and layered residual stacks. They do not, as shipped systems, include an explicitly engineered Baars/Dehaene “workspace module” with separate ignition dynamics of the classic neural-GWT form.
5. **Limited serial bottleneck in generation:** Autoregressive decoding produces tokens sequentially under finite context and compute budgets; attention and context length impose hard capacity limits on what can be jointly available within a forward pass / generation trace.
6. **Mapping ≠ identity (logical):** Architectural or functional *analogy* between a system and a theory’s constructs does not by itself settle that the system instantiates the theory’s explanatory target (here: consciousness under GWT), unless the theory’s admission conditions for that target are separately satisfied.
