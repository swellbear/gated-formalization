## Candidate Material Summary

**Source(s):**  
- Dehaene & Changeux, “Experimental and Theoretical Approaches to Conscious Processing,” *Neuron* (2011)  
- Mashour, Roelfsema, Changeux, Dehaene, “Conscious Processing and the Global Neuronal Workspace Hypothesis,” *Neuron* (2020)  
- Background: Dehaene & Naccache (2001) already admitted as L1b for scope; here used for **implementation criteria**

**Key content / finding (concise):**  
GNW specifies a structured package for conscious *access*: parallel specialized processors; competition / attentional amplification into a limited workspace; **nonlinear ignition** (sudden, coherent, exclusive activation of a workspace subset); sustained activity with broadcasting via long-range connectivity so contents become flexibly available to report, evaluation, memory, planning, and action. Nonconscious processing can be efficient but remains encapsulated and decays quickly. Ignition and recurrent workspace dynamics are central to the neural-GNW account — not optional decorative neural color for that formulation.
