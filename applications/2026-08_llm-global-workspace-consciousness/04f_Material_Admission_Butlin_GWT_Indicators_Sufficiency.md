## Candidate Material Summary

**Source(s):** Butlin, Long, et al., “Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence: Insights from the Science of Consciousness” (arXiv:2308.08708, 2023).  

**Key content / finding (concise):**  
Derives computational **indicator properties** from scientific theories rather than endorsing one theory as proven. For GWT specifically:
- **GWT-1:** Multiple specialised systems capable of operating in parallel (modules)
- **GWT-2:** Limited-capacity workspace (bottleneck + selective attention)
- **GWT-3:** Global broadcast (workspace information available to modules)
- **GWT-4:** State-dependent attention enabling successive module query for complex tasks  

Indicators raise/lower credence; the report does **not** treat any single checklist as settled joint sufficiency, and concludes **no current AI system is a strong candidate for consciousness**, while also judging there are no obvious technical barriers to building systems that satisfy many indicators. Transformer LLMs are discussed as partial/architecturally suggestive relative to GWT indicators, not as clearing them.
