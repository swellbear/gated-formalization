## Candidate Material Summary

**Source(s):** Ned Block, “On a Confusion about a Function of Consciousness,” *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* (1995). Secondary corroboration of the same distinction in later Block reviews (e.g., TiCS 2005: phenomenal content vs content broadcast in the global workspace).  

**Key content / finding (concise):**  
“Consciousness” is a mongrel concept. **Phenomenal consciousness (P)** = experience / what it is like. **Access consciousness (A)** = availability for reasoning and for rationally guiding speech and action (reportability as practical test). These are often conflated, with bad results. Access is a functional/system-relative notion; phenomenality is not defined as that functional role. Block later treats global-workspace broadcast as the characteristic mark of access content, not as automatic proof of P.
