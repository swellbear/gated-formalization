## Claim

Because the S&P 500 has historically delivered substantially lower average returns from May through October than from November through April, investors **should** be out of the S&P (or in cash/T-bills) for May–October; following the rule improves risk-adjusted results vs buy-and-hold over the long run and **should** be followed for May–October 2026.

---

## Plain verdict

**Seasonality is real under the agreed data lock** — average May–October returns have been substantially lower than November–April (about **3.5 percentage points** in the workbook). That fact does **not** mean investors should exit, that the rule beats buy-and-hold on risk-adjusted returns after costs/taxes, or that it must be followed in 2026. The Sharpe / “should” elevations **fail**.

---

## Established

- Seasonality ≠ obligation; long-run averages ≠ a 2026 mandate; risk-adjusted claims need locks.  
- Literature seasonality pattern exists; strategy superiority is not uniformly established in the literature.  
- Under the agreed lock: the seasonality gap clears the stated threshold (~3.52 pp ≥ 2.0 pp).  
- Lower strategy volatility appears but is not enough for a Sharpe win vs buy-and-hold.  
- Forced-deviation terms for the strong packaged slogan remain on record.  

---

## Not established

- Strategy Sharpe better than buy-and-hold after costs/taxes under the lock.  
- Default “should be out” and “should follow for May–October 2026.”  
- The full package: because seasonality → exit → improves risk-adjusted results → 2026 obligation.  

---

## Action implications

**Stop saying:** Historical Sell-in-May seasonality alone means investors **should** exit for May–Oct; that the rule **improves Sharpe vs buy-and-hold** after costs/taxes under the lock; or that it **must** be followed for May–Oct 2026.

**Keep saying:** Under post-1980s S&P total-return data as locked, average May–Oct returns have been **substantially lower** than Nov–Apr (~3.5 pp in the workbook); that is a seasonal fact, not an obligation or performance edge.

**Test next (only if authorized):** Revise to a descriptive-only claim; or a further tax-sensitivity check — else leave closed.
