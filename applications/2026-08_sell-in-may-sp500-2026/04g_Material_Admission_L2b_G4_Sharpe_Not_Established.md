## Candidate Material Summary

**Key content / finding (concise):**  
1. **Returns:** Large-Cap buy-and-hold mean return **higher** than switching (10.66% vs 7.90%); switching beats B&H on raw return in only **8/30** years; Wilcoxon favors B&H on returns for Large-Cap (significant).  
2. **Sharpe (pre-tax in study construction):** Large-Cap Sharpe difference switching vs B&H is **not significant** at 5% (Z ≈ 1.22, p ≈ 0.11). Study concludes results **lack statistical significance** to call switching **superior** to B&H.  
3. **Taxes (F3-relevant):** Switching realizes short-term gains; after-tax, study argues switching **should not be expected to outperform** B&H on a return basis in taxable accounts; tax friction **pressures** any risk-adjusted edge.
