## 2. Artifact summary
**Source / citation:**  
- https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Constitution/Article7  
- https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/200.001  

**What it reports (concise):**

Art. VII, s. 1(a): “No state ad valorem taxes shall be levied upon real estate or tangible personal property.” (State-level levy is already barred; Rank 1 object is **local** millage as a class.)

Art. VII, s. 9(a): “Counties, school districts, and municipalities shall, and special districts may, be authorized by law to levy ad valorem taxes …”

Art. VII, s. 9(b): millage **caps** (ten mills each for county, municipal, and school purposes, plus water-management and other special-district millage) — caps are not repeal.

F.S. 200.001 (2025): implements millage categories, including **general county millage**, **general municipal millage**, and school millages (nonvoted required/discretionary operating, capital, voted, debt service), plus dependent/independent special-district millage. Millages “shall be fixed only by ordinance or resolution of the governing body of the taxing authority.”

**Sample / setup limits:** Official compiled text, not a millage-rate print for every county. Census is **authority as a class**, not whether any one jurisdiction’s current millage is zero.
