## 2. Definition (USGA Stimpmeter)

Green speed = **average ball-roll distance (BRD) in feet** under the USGA Stimpmeter procedure.

- Official booklet Sommer cites: USGA, *Stimpmeter Instruction Booklet* (**2012**).
- **2013** update: two-sided Stimpmeter (**1X / 2X**) for shorter level areas.

Short paraphrase only. See [`COPY_GATE.md`](COPY_GATE.md). This is **not** the booklet.

---

## 3. Procedure (short paraphrase; not the booklet)

1. Choose a **level** area.
2. Roll **three** balls in one direction. Same-direction rests should cluster within **8 in**.
3. Roll **three** balls the **opposed** direction along the same line (**3+3 opposed**).
4. Green speed = **average of the two direction means**, in **feet**.

**8-inch same-direction cluster** = USGA **validation tolerance**. It is **not** Amb **X**. Do **not** vanity-freeze 8 in as the handheld / casual-putt bar.
