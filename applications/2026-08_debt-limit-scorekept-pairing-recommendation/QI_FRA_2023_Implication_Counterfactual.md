## 3. Gated evaluation of conditioned benefit (B)

### L3a — Stipulated balance by construction (ADMIT, counterfactual)

**Material:** Under the Rank 1 freeze, C* ≥ H is exactly the definition of “balanced.” So in the counterfactual where C ≈ $4.7T non-interest cuts, FRA **would satisfy** the stipulated balance criterion.

- [x] **ADMIT** as **L3a (counterfactual / descriptive):** Conditioned on meeting the standard, the **stipulated** fiscal-balance benefit follows **by construction**.  
**Must not promote to:** Substantive unrestricted fiscal responsibility; soft “should”; parent FD clearance.

### L3b — Substantive “fiscally responsible” not forced (ADMIT negative)

**Material:** L₀ anchors and prior L1c/L1d: evaluative fiscal responsibility depends on criteria beyond the stipulated test (macro path, composition, default/disruption tradeoffs, revenues, interest, timing). A $4.7T cut package could be scored “balanced” while remaining contested as good policy.

- [x] **ADMIT** as **L3b (counterfactual / negative):** Meeting C ≥ H does **not** force unrestricted “fiscally responsible.”

### L3c — Soft “should” not forced by counterfactual meet (ADMIT negative)

**Material:** Even if FRA had met the standard, that does not by itself establish that Congress *should* use this standard as a standing rule, nor that soft “should” is generally warranted (G1 remains open).

- [x] **ADMIT** as **L3c (counterfactual / negative):** Counterfactual meet does **not** close G1.

### L3d — Headroom vs decade cuts are different objects (ADMIT honesty)

**Material:** H measures suspension borrowing capacity actually used to finance obligations; C* measures scored decade outlay reductions. Equality under the lock is a **magnitude match**, not an identity of cash-flows in the suspension window. Counterfactual “balance” remains a scorekeeping verdict, not a proof of unchanged debt or avoided all fiscal risk.

- [x] **ADMIT** as **L3d (counterfactual / structural honesty):** Benefit that follows by construction is the **stipulated scorekept match**, not stronger macroeconomic identity claims.
