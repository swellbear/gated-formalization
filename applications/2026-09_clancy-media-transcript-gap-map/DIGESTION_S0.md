## 2. Day-page class (short paraphrase)

A **Rev (or similar) day page** is a public, machine-aided **unofficial** transcript of a **media day** audio/video. Typical page objects: speaker labels, timestamps, `[inaudible]` markers.

- Vendor class: **Rev** (and similar) **media / unofficial**.
- What the page *is*: unofficial media-day text.
- What the page is **not**: the **certified court-reporter record**. Not an official Massachusetts Superior Court transcript. Not a verdict. Not legal advice.

Bibliographic starter-set day pages (Rev URLs + Rev page stamps) sit on [`SOURCE.md`](SOURCE.md) §2. Rev page dates are **media-date stamps**, not a certified court calendar.
