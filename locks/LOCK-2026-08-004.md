## Locked content

Many claims in domains that are typically **hybrid or spectrum-like** (including design/architecture, payment methods, and similar) are not pure binaries. Assertions of the form “Option A alone avoids failure mode F” or “only A avoids F” usually require early forced-deviation extraction: what exact deviation from the pure form would re-introduce F, and is that deviation already present in real instances of A?
