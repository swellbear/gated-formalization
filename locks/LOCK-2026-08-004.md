## Locked content

Many design and architecture claims are spectra, not binary. Assertions of the form “Approach A alone structurally cannot fail mode F” or “only A avoids F” usually require early forced-deviation extraction: what exact deviation from the pure form would re-introduce F, and is that deviation already present in real instances of A?

---

## Original free parameter(s) closed

Treating design approaches as pure binary categories when they are typically continuous or hybrid.
