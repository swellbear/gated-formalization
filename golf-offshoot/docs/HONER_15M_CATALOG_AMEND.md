# honer_15m catalog amendment protocol

**This build does not add a third family.** Two dated items only: `H-SKIP-RICH-YES` then `H-SKIP-WIDE-SPREAD`. Both hunt **in parallel**. Lab may date a third family only after both of those exams finish (dead or catalog exhaust). Not while they are still running.

A new family is not a tape sort, a pnl rank, or a Founder-free append.

## Order

1. **Lab** — dated PROPOSED in a later session. Names the family id, knob, start, clip, step, and the file-derived `activate` (today: `start`, `clip_exhaustion`, `quote_quality_ok`, `search_starvation`, `clip_exhaustion+quote_quality_ok`, `search_starvation+quote_quality_ok`, or an `|` of those named gates). Parallel hunts use `start`.
2. **Operator** — RUN-ONLY on that note. Does not ADMIT. Does not bind. Does not score.
3. **Soften Critic** — a **later** session from the author of the PROPOSED. Not this implement session. Not on the factory clerical whitelist.

File order **is** the exam queue (catalog family, then clip). `catalog.next_family` walks `items[]` as written. Do not sort from the tape. Do not rank from pnl.

## Activate must be file-derived

`activate` is a named gate already on disk (`start`, `clip_exhaustion`, `quote_quality_ok`, `search_starvation`). It is not a discretionary switch.

Family 1 and family 2 both use **`start`**. They hunt on the same sidecar tick. Family 2 does **not** wait on family-1 clip exhaustion or second starvation. Incomplete quotes skip that spread hunt this tick; do not invent a spread.

The dated strings are:

`start`

`search_starvation` remains a named reachability gate on a hunt's own clock. It does not start the other family. It does not revive a burned class. Soften Critic of a later third-family activate is a **later** session.

## Burn list (refuse these ids)

FLIP, PERSIST, SEAS-DIR, SHRINK, LOGIT, RETUNE-COINFLIP-BAND, FEE-AS-SIGNAL, SUM-LINEAGES, BACKFILL-GAP, BASELINE-AS-EDGE.

`catalog.py` rejects a burned id. Do not revive them under a new spelling to skip this list.

## Required fields

Every item: `declared_at`, `id`, and `activate` (the first item may omit `activate`; that means start). Missing `declared_at` is a CatalogError.

## This build

Does **not** add a third item. Does not score, bind, or arm. Search starvation is honer-only; it does not become a factory selection rule. Dating clip hunts is not a trial.

When `library.json` `catalog_exhausted` is true, the sidecar **parks search** (no new search fills) and leaves the exam closed. `latest/family_amend.json` stamps `HONER-FAMILY-AMEND` owed from those files (`catalog_exhausted` or exam `completed_dead`). The doorbell does not read exam pnl. Lab dating a third family is a later session.
