## Rivals vs complements

Same kind, different named slots of one product partition (skip `:00` vs `:15` vs `:30` vs `:45`) are **rivals**. They wait in a queue. One takes the chair. AND-skip of a full quartet is fill-none.

Different kinds whose skip sets do not cover every window may **named-stack** after a keeper (tidy stack). Do not stack mid-look. Do not glue a complement onto a parked corpse.

Farm L1 pass means eligible for the queue or a later named stack. The next live 70 is the confirmation. Growing the farm does not mint official `trials_to_date`.
