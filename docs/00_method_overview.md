# Gated Progressive Formalization — Core Method (Living Overview)

*Living markdown version. Archival PDF remains the frozen complete thesis.*

## 1. Purpose

Convert messy conceptual material (metaphors, informal theories, advocacy arguments, design rationales, etc.) into clearer, better-constrained models under explicit discipline. The method surfaces under-determination, blocks unconstrained elaboration, and turns identified gaps into a research agenda. It does not eliminate evaluative judgment.

## 2. Core Gated Process

**L₀** = objective anchors (empirical results, logical constraints, strong intersubjective facts).

A new layer **Lₖ₊₁** is admitted only if **all** of the following hold:

1. **Cons** (Consistency) with L₀ ∪ prior layers ≥ τ_c
2. **Agree** (Agreement / Stability) ≥ τ_a
3. **Prod** (Productivity) ≥ τ_p
4. **Amb** (Ambiguity / Under-determination) ≤ τ_u
5. Higher-level review against a fixed rubric passes

**Revision rule**: Any earlier layer may be updated or withdrawn if inconsistency appears. Revisions must themselves re-satisfy the gates.

**Required annotations** for every admitted layer:
- Claims now constrained by anchors
- Claims that remain free or under-determined
- Estimated reliability of the expansion/revision step
- Any contradictions that forced revision of earlier layers

## 3. Gap-Directed Search Loop

When a gate fails (especially Amb):

1. Extract the specific free parameters / missing constraints.
2. Actively search for material that targets those exact gaps.
3. Evaluate candidate material for **relevance to the targeted gap** + **consistency** with current anchors and layers. Admit only material that clears both.
4. Incorporate clearing material and re-score.
5. Repeat until gates clear **or** the search returns nothing that meaningfully reduces the failure → remain provisional and stop expanding the formal structure.

## 4. Supporting Procedures (Higher-Order Relevance & Importance)

- **Gap ranking heuristics** — score gaps by impact, anchor connection, and measurability.
- **Search-completeness rules** — declare source classes and a diminishing-returns / time-box rule; record what was checked.
- **Anchor stress-testing** — seek potential defeating evidence or alternative anchors and re-score.
- **Comparative direction evaluation** — run alternative formalization paths under the same gates.
- **Classical sound-argument training for relevance** — use historically successful arguments as exemplars to condition relevance judgment (does not constitute proof).

## 5. Residual Judgment (Explicit)

Judgment remains at: choice of anchors, threshold calibration, relevance decisions, gap ranking, declaring search sufficient, assessing overall significance, selecting classical exemplars, borderline calls, and deciding that a consequence is non-trivial. The method scaffolds these points; it does not remove them.

## 6. Under-determination vs. Falsehood

High residual Ambiguity after serious search means the claim is **not yet well-constrained** (provisional / theoretical relative to current anchors). It does **not** prove the claim false.

Clear signs of falsehood require a hard, stable Cons failure: direct incompatibility that survives scrutiny, converges across independent sources, produces predictive failure, cannot be restored by legitimate revision, and forces alternative readings to collapse.

## 7. What the Method Claims and Does Not Claim

**Does**: makes under-determination visible; constrains unconstrained elaboration; forces residual judgment into the open; converts gaps into a research agenda; supplies improved heuristics for relevance.

**Does not**: eliminate judgment; prove relevance in a strong external sense; guarantee the most important gap or best material has been found; supply a general algorithm for insight.
