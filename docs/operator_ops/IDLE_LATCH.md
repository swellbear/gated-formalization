# Idle latch (full)

Habit §F. Short list: [`templates/OPERATOR_IDLE_LATCH.md`](../../templates/OPERATOR_IDLE_LATCH.md). Handshake: habit §C.

Idle ON means the live Softened set is closed until a **fresh Founder GO that names the next invent**.

## When idle is ON, both files say so

Write the latch in **both**:

1. `SOFTENED_SET.md` — **Idle ON** + the reject rule.
2. Operator STATUS stamp — **Operator idle until Founder GO? Y** + the reject rule.

The reject rule in one line: *any new Lab PROPOSED under idle is idle-breach REJECT, not Softened.*

Chat-only idle is not the latch.

## Enforcement

- Any new Lab **PROPOSED** under idle → **REJECT as idle-breach**. The board is **not Softened**.
- **Soften Hard NO** under idle. Do not Soften the breach to absorb chatter, to “keep the lane moving,” or to clear a Digestor CONFLICT.
- CONFLICT on an idle-breach PROPOSED clears by **parking** the board (REJECTED/PARKED), not by Softening it.
- Do **not** re-reply park STATUS to pings that only restate the same facts (habit §C). Restate ≠ GO.

## Clearing idle

Clear idle **only** on a fresh Founder **GO** that **names the next invent**.

Example (golf): WC3+ **on a new settled week**. Not “continue weekly.” Not “Lab already wrote WC2.” Not option-A settle hygiene on a later banner.

Until that GO:

- Idle stays ON.
- Softened set stays the already-admitted set (example: WC1-only).
- New weekly invents stay idle-breach.

## Worked example (golf 2026-09-07)

WC1 dated record ADMITTED (FAIL / park unproven; edge not established). Idle ON. Lab invented WC2 without a fresh Founder GO naming that invent. Founder rejected WC2 as idle-breach. Stamp: **PARKED/REJECTED — not Softened**. Idle stayed ON (WC3+ only on a new settled week). Digestor CONFLICT cleared by the park, not by a WC2 Soften.

## What this is not

- Not a Soften of the rejected board.
- Not a Soften Critic hire (the critic is already hired; the idle latch does not hire it).
- Not an auto-GO of C2/C4 or of the next weekly invent.
- Not a banked-edge or “edge established” stamp.
