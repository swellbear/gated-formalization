---

## `forced-deviation`

**Definition:** No Minimal-deviation lock package; over-strong / under-specified claim terms cannot be tested in non-derivative form.

**Applications:** debt-limit parent · SpaceX · AV E2E · many-worlds · sell-in-May · llm-gwt (sufficiency/scale as over-strong elevations)

**Lesson:** Extract FD terms early; Amb drop from locks ≠ salvage of bare slogan.

---

## `R-dependence`

**Definition:** Dependent questions blocked primarily by an unset lock or free parameter (inter-parameter dependency).

**Applications:** AV E2E (R1/R2 ← R4/OR-slots) · many-worlds (A3–A4 ← A1/A2) · SpaceX (M2 ← Rank 1 locks) · conscience (expansion ← free f/Pi/R)

**Lesson:** State dependency explicitly; locking-scaffolding before pretending empirical closure.

---

## `comparison-class-unset`

**Definition:** Uniqueness or preferability blocked because comparison class / metrics / ODD unset.

**Applications:** AV E2E · many-worlds

**Lesson:** Preferability/uniqueness without a locked comparison class is not well-posed.

---

## `sharpe-after-cost-fail`

**Definition:** Claimed risk-adjusted improvement fails once costs/taxes (or even pre-tax Sharpe) are checked under lock.

**Applications:** sell-in-May (Rank 1 F3 workbook)

**Lesson:** Seasonality (or other descriptive edge) can hold while Sharpe-after-cost fails — already-included legs do not rescue the elevation.

---

## `C-lt-H-fail`

**Definition:** Named instance fails numerical C ≥ H (or stipulated equality) under freeze.

**Applications:** debt-limit Rank 1 (FRA 2023) · debt-limit parent (instance reject / no public C≥H pass)

**Lesson:** Well-posed numerical standards can fail cleanly; failure ≠ unrestricted “irresponsible.”

---

## `QI-scale-factor`

**Definition:** Quantitative Implication mode run after failed numerical instance.

**Applications:** debt-limit Rank 1 (FRA ~3.6×; stipulated CF only)

**Lesson:** Scale-factor / counterfactual labels are implications — not proof of soft “should.”

---

## `contrastive-alternatives`

**Definition:** Contrastive Recommendation mode after established core + failed elevation.

**Applications:** sell-in-May (Alt A–C)

**Lesson:** Offer better-fitting alternatives without silently rewriting the original claim.

---

## `parent-successor-family`

**Definition:** Claim-Revision lineage across folders.

**Applications:** debt-limit equal-spending-cuts → scorekept-pairing-recommendation

**Lesson:** Successor findings do not overwrite parent FD/closeout.

---

## `incomplete-record`

**Definition:** Worksheets/closeout missing; reconstruction from verdict/dissertation only.

**Applications:** foxnews-socialism-article · conscience-sketch

**Lesson:** Mark incomplete honestly; recover records before elevating residuals.
